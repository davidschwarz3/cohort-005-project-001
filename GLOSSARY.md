# Cadence

Cadence is a course platform where Learners buy courses and watch their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys courses and watches their lessons.
_Avoid_: Student, customer, buyer

### Refunds

**Refund Reviewer**:
The Cadence admin who approves or declines a Refund Request. A course's instructor is never the Refund Reviewer.
_Avoid_: Our team, someone, support

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's ask to have a Purchase refunded, with their reason. It is always in one of three states: requested, approved or declined. Learners see these as "Refund requested", "Refund approved" and "Refund declined".
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, refund (for the ask itself); raised, pending, done (as status words)

**Refund**:
Money returned to the Learner when a Refund Request is approved. A Refund always covers the whole Purchase, never part of it, and it removes the Learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Cancellation, Chargeback, partial refund

**Refund Amount**:
What a Refund pays back: always the amount the Learner paid for the Purchase, never the course's list price.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Refund Window**:
The period from the purchase day through the 14th calendar day after it, inclusive. A Learner may raise a Refund Request only during this period.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
The one set of conditions under which a Learner may raise a Refund Request:
- They have a Purchase of the course.
- That Purchase has never had a Refund Request. A decline is final.
- Today is inside the Refund Window.
- They have watched less than 2 hours of the course in total.
- They have completed less than half of its lessons.
- The Purchase is not a team-licence seat that has been claimed. A team licence is refundable only while none of its seats has been claimed.

_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute the Learner raises with their bank or card issuer, outside Cadence. It is never a Refund Request.
