# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around buying seats for others and getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member. Admins are the only people who decide Refund Requests, and an Admin never decides their own.
_Avoid_: the team, Team Admin (a Team Admin is a buyer, not staff), Instructor

### Teams

**Team**:
The Seats one buyer has bought for other people.
_Avoid_: Licence, Organisation

**Team Admin**:
The buyer who owns a Team. They hand out its Seats and can ask for money back on the ones nobody has claimed.
_Avoid_: Admin (Cadence staff)

**Seat**:
One place on a course, bought by a Team Admin, that one person can claim.
_Avoid_: Coupon (for the place itself), Licence

**Claimed Seat**:
A Seat someone has taken. It's theirs to learn from, and it can't be refunded: not by them, because they paid nothing, and not by the Team Admin, because someone is using it.
_Avoid_: Redeemed

**Unclaimed Seat**:
A Seat nobody has taken yet. A Team Admin can hand Unclaimed Seats back for a Refund.

**Coupon**:
The code or link a person uses to claim a Seat. It's only the link, never the Seat itself.
_Avoid_: Seat, Voucher

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase: either their own purchase of a course, or one or more Unclaimed Seats from a Team. An Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a learner may raise a Refund Request on a purchase. For their own purchase of a course, they must be within fourteen calendar days of buying, have watched under two hours, and have completed under half the lessons. Someone who claimed a Seat paid nothing, so they can never raise one. Eligibility is judged when the request is raised, not when an Admin decides it.

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund. The learner loses access to the course and their rating of it is removed the moment it's approved, not when the money arrives. Their progress is kept, so if they buy the course again they pick up where they left off. A new purchase is judged on Refund Eligibility like any other, and time already watched still counts.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down. The Admin always gives the learner a reason, so they know what to change if they raise a new Refund Request. It has no Refund.

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount paid for what's being returned: the whole price of their own purchase, or the price of each Unclaimed Seat handed back. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund from an approved Refund Request where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet. Cadence doesn't send the money itself: an Admin sends it outside Cadence, then confirms whether it went through.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where an Admin has confirmed the money was sent back to the learner. Once a Refund is Issued it stays Issued.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Admin records why, for other Admins to see. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the Refund becomes Issued once an Admin sends the money successfully. The learner sees it as still on its way.
